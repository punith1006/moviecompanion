# CINEPAL - Product Requirements Document

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**One-liner**: The first AI-powered entertainment companion that remembers your watchlist, generates smart recaps, and makes content discovery a conversational delight—not a chore.

**Problem**: Students and entertainment enthusiasts struggle with fragmented viewing experiences: they forget what happened in ongoing series, receive repetitive or irrelevant recommendations from streaming platforms, and have no single intelligent companion that truly understands their preferences and enriches their entertainment journey.

**Solution**: CinePal is a conversational AI companion built with Google Agent Development Kit that combines persistent memory (MongoDB), adaptive reasoning, and interactive engagement. Unlike static tracking apps, CinePal converses naturally, generates contextual recaps, filters out already-watched and rejected content, and gamifies the experience with character quizzes—creating a 10x more engaging entertainment assistant.

**Target Users**:
- **Primary**: College students (18-25) who watch 10+ hours of content weekly across multiple platforms
- **Secondary**: Casual binge-watchers (25-35) overwhelmed by streaming choice paralysis
- **Tertiary**: Anime/K-drama enthusiasts who track complex storylines across seasons

**Market Opportunity**:
- TAM: $2.4B global entertainment tracking/discovery market
- SAM: $400M English-speaking students and young professionals segment
- SOM: $4M (1% penetration in first year)

**Competitive Advantage**:
- 🧠 **Memory-First Architecture**: Unlike Trakt/Letterboxd that just log, CinePal remembers preferences, dislikes, and partial progress
- 💬 **Conversational AI**: No forms, no clicking—just ask "What should I watch tonight?"
- 🎮 **Gamified Engagement**: Quote quizzes, trivia, and fun facts keep users returning

## 2. PRODUCT VISION & STRATEGY

### 2.1 Innovation Thesis

Traditional entertainment tracking apps treat users as passive data entry clerks—click to add, scroll to find, repeat. CinePal inverts this paradigm by positioning AI as an active companion that remembers, reasons, and engages.

The core insight is that entertainment consumption is emotional, not transactional. When you pick a movie to watch on Friday night, you're not executing a database query—you're seeking an experience that matches your mood, available time, and social context. CinePal is built to understand this nuanced decision-making process through conversational AI that learns your preferences while making the journey itself enjoyable.

By combining Google's Agent Development Kit with MongoDB's flexible schema for memory, we create a system that maintains context across sessions, avoids recommendation fatigue by explicitly filtering rejected content, and transforms passive tracking into active engagement through quizzes and trivia.

### 2.2 User Personas

#### Primary Persona: Alex - The Overwhelmed Binger
- **Demographics**: 22-year-old college student, tech-savvy, watches content during study breaks
- **Goals**: Find something great to watch in under 2 minutes, keep track of 5+ ongoing series
- **Pain Points**: Forgets plot points between seasons, gets trapped in recommendation loops, wastes time scrolling
- **Behaviors**: Uses Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video; browses Reddit for recommendations; uses notes app to track shows
- **Success Metrics**: Time to find next show <60 seconds, never recommends already-watched content
- **Quote**: "I just spent 30 minutes looking for something to watch and ended up rewatching The Office again."

#### Secondary Persona: Priya - The K-Drama Enthusiast  
- **Demographics**: 28-year-old working professional, follows Asian dramas and anime
- **Goals**: Track complex multi-season storylines, discover hidden gems outside mainstream
- **Pain Points**: Mainstream apps don't track Asian content well, forgets character names between episodes
- **Behaviors**: Uses Viki, Crunchyroll, Netflix; active in fan communities; maintains spreadsheets
- **Success Metrics**: Accurate tracking of Asian content, recaps that include character names
- **Quote**: "I need someone to remind me who that character is before each episode."

#### Tertiary Persona: Marcus - The Social Watcher
- **Demographics**: 35-year-old, watches with partner, needs family-friendly recommendations
- **Goals**: Find content both partners enjoy, avoid inappropriate surprise content
- **Pain Points**: Joint watchlist management is clunky, profiles don't sync preferences
- **Behaviors**: Uses shared streaming accounts, discusses shows at work
- **Success Metrics**: Recommendations suitable for couple viewing, easy shared history
- **Quote**: "We both liked that one show—what else would we both enjoy?"

### 2.3 Value Proposition Canvas

**Jobs to be Done**:
- Functional: Find relevant content quickly, remember what was watched, catch up on storylines
- Emotional: Feel in control of entertainment choices, reduce decision anxiety
- Social: Share recommendations with friends, discuss shows knowledgeably

**Pain Relievers**:
- Eliminates repetitive recommendations by tracking dislikes and already-watched
- Removes scroll fatigue through conversational recommendations
- Prevents "where was I?" confusion with AI-generated recaps

**Gain Creators**:
- Makes discovery fun through quizzes and trivia
- Creates personalized experience that improves over time
- Provides instant recap "warm-up" before returning to a series

## 3. PRODUCT FEATURES & REQUIREMENTS

### 3.1 Feature Prioritization Matrix

#### MUST-HAVE FEATURES (MVP Core)

---

**Feature 1: Conversational AI Chat Interface**

- **User Story**: As a user, I need to ask natural language questions about what to watch so that I can get personalized recommendations without navigating complex filters.

- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - [ ] Users can type natural language queries like "What should I watch tonight?" or "Something funny and under 2 hours"
  - [ ] AI responds with 3-5 contextual recommendations with brief reasoning
  - [ ] Multi-turn conversations maintain context within a session
  - [ ] Response time <3 seconds for recommendations

- **User Flow**:
  1. User lands on chat interface (primary screen)
  2. Sees welcome message with example prompts
  3. Types question in natural language
  4. AI processes query against user history and preferences
  5. Returns formatted recommendations with posters, ratings, and streaming availability
  6. User can ask follow-up questions or select a title for more info

- **UI/UX Requirements**:
  - Chat bubble interface with distinct user/AI styling
  - Recommendation cards with cover art (200x300px), title, year, rating, and streaming icon
  - Typing indicator animation during AI processing
  - Quick action buttons: "Add to Watchlist", "Mark as Watched", "Not Interested"

- **Technical Requirements**:
  - FastAPI endpoint: `POST /api/ai/chat`
  - Integration with OpenAI/Claude API for natural language understanding
  - MongoDB query integration for user history context
  - Caching layer for common recommendation patterns

- **Success Metric**: 80% of users make at least 3 conversational queries in first session

- **Demo Impact**: Immediate "wow" factor—users can just ask, no learning curve

---

**Feature 2: Smart Watch History & Memory**

- **User Story**: As a user, I need the system to remember everything I've watched, liked, and disliked so that it never recommends content I've already seen or explicitly rejected.

- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - [ ] Users can mark content as: Watched, Currently Watching (with episode progress), Dropped, Not Interested
  - [ ] Watch history persists across sessions via MongoDB
  - [ ] AI recommendations automatically exclude watched/rejected content
  - [ ] Users can view and edit their complete history

- **User Flow**:
  1. User searches or receives recommendation
  2. Clicks status button to mark (quick action)
  3. For "Currently Watching," prompted to select last episode watched
  4. History updates in real-time
  5. Future recommendations reflect this data

- **UI/UX Requirements**:
  - Status pills with icons (eye=watched, pause=watching, X=dropped, thumbs-down=not interested)
  - Progress bar for ongoing series showing episodes watched
  - History page with filters by status, genre, date added
  - Swipe-to-mark on mobile for quick logging

- **Technical Requirements**:
  - MongoDB Schema: `WatchHistory { userId, contentId, status, episodeProgress, dateAdded, dateUpdated }`
  - API endpoints: `GET/POST/PUT /api/history`
  - Indexed queries for fast history lookup during recommendation generation

- **Success Metric**: Zero recommendations of already-watched content after first 10 interactions

- **Demo Impact**: Shows the "memory" differentiator—system gets smarter with use

---

**Feature 3: AI-Powered Episode Recaps**

- **User Story**: As a user returning to an ongoing series, I need a quick recap of what happened in previous episodes so that I can jump back in without confusion.

- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - [ ] Users can request "Recap of [Show Name]" via chat or button
  - [ ] AI generates spoiler-conscious recap up to user's current episode
  - [ ] Recap covers key plot points, character developments, cliffhangers
  - [ ] Loading time <5 seconds for recap generation

- **User Flow**:
  1. User navigates to "Currently Watching" section or asks in chat
  2. Clicks "Get Recap" button on a show card
  3. System identifies user's progress (e.g., "You're on Season 2, Episode 5")
  4. AI generates contextual recap of S1 through S2E4
  5. Displays recap with optional TL;DR toggle

- **UI/UX Requirements**:
  - Accordion-style recap with season/episode breakdowns
  - TL;DR mode (2-3 sentences) vs Full Recap toggle
  - Character name highlights with optional "who is this?" tooltips
  - Spoiler warning before showing content past user's current episode

- **Technical Requirements**:
  - FastAPI endpoint: `POST /api/ai/recap`
  - Integration with content databases (TMDb, IMDb) for episode data
  - LLM prompt engineering for spoiler-aware recap generation
  - Caching recaps per show/episode combination

- **Success Metric**: 60% of returning users request at least one recap per week

- **Demo Impact**: Unique feature—no competitor offers this at scale

---

**Feature 4: Content Search & Discovery**

- **User Story**: As a user, I need to search for specific movies or shows so that I can add them to my watchlist or mark them as watched.

  4. Detail modal/page shows: synopsis, rating, cast, streaming availability, user actions

- **Technical Requirements**:

- **Demo Impact**: Proves the comprehensive database behind the AI

### 3.2 Critical User Journeys

#### Journey 1: First-Time User Experience (FTUE)

**Goal**: Deliver value in <60 seconds

**Flow**:

1. **Landing Page**
   - Screen: Hero section with animated chat demo
   - Copy: "Your AI Entertainment Companion" / "Tell me what you love—I'll remember forever."
   - CTA: "Start Chatting Free" (primary), "See How It Works" (secondary)
   - Design Note: Dark gradient background (#0f0d17 to #1a1625), floating movie poster elements

2. **Quick Onboarding (30 seconds)**
   - Approach: Conversational onboarding in chat interface itself
   - Data Collected: 3 favorite shows/movies (suggested via autocomplete)
   - AI Response: "Great taste! Based on these, I already have 5 ideas for you. Want to see them?"

3. **Aha Moment** (~45 seconds from start)
   - Trigger: AI presents first batch of personalized recommendations
   - Experience: "I noticed you like dark thrillers—here's something you probably haven't seen..."
   - User sees recommendations they haven't watched

4. **First Core Action**
   - What: User adds item to watchlist or marks as "Already Watched"
   - Friction Points Eliminated: No complex forms, just quick action buttons
   - Success State: Confirmation with "I've noted that! Now I'll never recommend it again."

5. **Retention Hook**
   - Why Return: Daily quiz notification, new recommendations based on growing profile
   - Mechanism: Streak counter ("You're on a 3-day streak!"), email digest (weekly)

**Edge Cases & Error Handling**:
- User inputs unknown title → AI asks for clarification or offers closest matches
- API rate limit hit → Show cached recommendations with "Refreshing in a moment..."
- Network error during onboarding → Local storage preserves progress, sync on reconnect

---

#### Journey 2: Getting a Recap for an Ongoing Series

**Goal**: Catch up on a show in under 30 seconds

**Flow**:

1. User asks: "Catch me up on Stranger Things" or clicks "Get Recap" on show card
2. System checks user's progress: "You're on Season 4, Episode 2"
3. AI generates recap: "Here's what happened from Season 1 through Season 4 Episode 1..."
4. User reads TL;DR or expands for full recap

---

#### Journey 3: Finding Something to Watch Tonight

**Goal**: Get a great recommendation in under 60 seconds

**Flow**:

1. User opens app → Chat greeting: "Hey! What are you in the mood for tonight?"
2. User types: "Something funny, not too long, I'm tired"

## 4. USER EXPERIENCE & DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### 4.1 Design Philosophy

**Core Emotion**: Empowered, Delighted, Understood

**Brand Personality**: If CinePal was a person, they'd be a knowledgeable friend who loves movies, never judges your guilty pleasures, and always has a great recommendation ready. Warm but smart, playful but reliable.

**Visual Metaphors**: A cozy movie night (warm, inviting), a personal concierge (helpful, attentive), a trivia night (fun, engaging)

**Competitive Visual Differentiation**: While Letterboxd is artsy-minimalist and Trakt is data-dense, CinePal embraces approachable warmth with conversational UI and subtle animations.

### 4.2 Design System

#### Color Palette

**Dark Mode (Primary)**:
- **Background Primary**: `#0f0d17` - Deep space black with purple undertone - Main app background
- **Background Secondary**: `#1a1625` - Elevated surfaces, cards
- **Background Tertiary**: `#252033` - Input fields, hover states

**Accent Colors**:
- **Primary**: `#8b5cf6` (Violet 500) - CTAs, selected states, interactive elements
- **Primary Hover**: `#a78bfa` (Violet 400) - Hover states
- **Secondary**: `#f43f5e` (Rose 500) - Notifications, badges, hearts
- **Success**: `#22c55e` (Green 500) - Watched status, confirmations
- **Warning**: `#f59e0b` (Amber 500) - In-progress, partial states
- **Error**: `#ef4444` (Red 500) - Errors, remove actions

**Text Colors**:
- **Heading**: `#ffffff` - 100% white for headers
- **Body**: `#e2e1e7` - Slightly muted for readability
- **Muted**: `#a09dab` - Secondary text, timestamps
- **Disabled**: `#6b6873` - Inactive states

**Rationale**: Deep purple-black backgrounds evoke the magic of a movie theater. Violet accents feel modern and premium without the corporate blue of productivity apps.

#### Typography

**Font Stack**:
- **Display/Headlines**: `'Outfit', sans-serif` (Google Fonts) - Weights: 600, 700
  - H1: 48px/56px (landing), 32px/40px (in-app)
  - H2: 24px/32px
  - H3: 20px/28px
  
- **Body**: `'Inter', sans-serif` (Google Fonts) - Weights: 400, 500, 600
  - Body Large: 18px/28px
  - Body: 16px/24px
  - Body Small: 14px/20px

- **UI/Captions**: `'Inter', sans-serif` - Weight: 500
  - Button: 14px/20px, letter-spacing: 0.02em
  - Caption: 12px/16px

- **Code/Data**: `'JetBrains Mono', monospace` - For any data display

#### Spacing System

- Base unit: 4px
- Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128
- Card padding: 16px (small), 24px (default), 32px (hero)
- Section gaps: 48px (between major sections), 24px (within sections)

#### Iconography Approach

- **Style**: Outlined with 1.5px stroke weight, rounded caps
- **Source**: Lucide React (customized) - NOT generic FontAwesome
- **Design Direction**: Rounded, friendly, consistent 24x24px sizing
- **Implementation**: React components for full color/size control
- **Key Icons Needed**:
  - Chat bubble (conversation)
  - Eye (watched)
  - Clock (in progress)
  - Heart (favorite)
  - Bookmark (watchlist)
  - Play (streaming link)
  - Brain (AI/smart features)
  - Trophy (quiz/gamification)

#### Component Library

**Using**: shadcn/ui as base with heavy customization

**Buttons**:
- Primary: Violet gradient background, white text, 12px radius
- Secondary: Ghost/outline style, violet border
- Destructive: Rose background for remove/delete
- States: Hover (lift + glow), Active (pressed), Loading (spinner)

**Cards**:
- Content cards: 12px radius, subtle border (#252033), hover lift with shadow
- Chat bubbles: User (violet bg, right aligned), AI (secondary bg, left aligned)

**Inputs**:
- Rounded (12px radius), dark background
- Focus: Violet ring with glow
- Error: Red border with shake animation

**Modals/Dialogs**:
- Centered, fade + scale animation
- Dark overlay (black 80% opacity)
- Close button top-right

### 4.3 Micro-Interactions & Animations

**Philosophy**: Motion should guide attention, provide feedback, and add personality—never distract.

**Key Animations**:

- **Loading States**: 
  - Skeleton loading with shimmer effect for cards
  - AI typing indicator: 3 bouncing dots, violet color
  
- **Success Confirmations**: 
  - Checkmark draw animation (0.3s)
  - Subtle confetti burst for quiz completion
  
- **Error Handling**: 
  - Input shake (200ms, 3 oscillations)
  - Red border fade-in
  
- **Transitions**: 
  - Page transitions: Fade (200ms) + subtle slide (10px)
  - Modal: Scale from 95% + fade (200ms)
  
- **Hover States**: 
  - Cards: Lift (translateY -4px) + shadow increase
  - Buttons: Subtle glow + color shift
  
- **Empty States**: 
  - Custom illustration (simple, line-art style)
  - Encouraging copy: "Your watchlist is empty—ask me for recommendations!"

**Performance Budget**: Max animation duration 400ms, 60fps minimum

### 4.4 Responsive Design Strategy

- **Breakpoints**:
  - Mobile: <640px (single column, full-width chat)
  - Tablet: 640-1024px (two columns where applicable)
  - Desktop: >1024px (sidebar + main content layout)

- **Mobile-First Considerations**:
  - Touch targets minimum 44x44px
  - Bottom navigation for key actions
  - Chat input fixed at bottom
  - Swipe gestures for quick actions

### 4.5 Accessibility Standards

- [ ] WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- [ ] Color contrast: 4.5:1 minimum for text
- [ ] Keyboard navigation for all interactions
- [ ] Screen reader announcements for dynamic content
- [ ] Focus indicators (2px violet ring)
- [ ] Alt text for all images and posters
- [ ] Error messages: Clear, actionable, persistent
- [ ] Reduced motion preference respected

### 4.6 Content Strategy & Voice

**Tone Attributes**: Friendly, Knowledgeable, Playful

**Writing Principles**:
- Conversational, not robotic ("I found something you'll love" not "Results found")
- Brief but warm ("Great pick!" not just "Added")

#### API Endpoints Specification

**Authentication**

`POST /api/auth/register`
- Request: `{ email, password, name }`
- Response (201): `{ user: { id, email, name }, accessToken, refreshToken }`
- Errors: 400 (validation), 409 (email exists)

`POST /api/auth/login`
- Request: `{ email, password }`
- Response (200): `{ user, accessToken, refreshToken }`
- Errors: 401 (invalid credentials)

`POST /api/auth/refresh`
- Request: (refreshToken in httpOnly cookie)
- Response (200): `{ accessToken }`

`POST /api/auth/logout`
- Response (200): `{ success: true }`

**Watch History**

`GET /api/history`
- Query: `?status=watched&page=1&limit=20`
- Response: Paginated history items

`POST /api/history`
- Request: `{ contentId, contentType, status, episodeProgress? }`
- Response (201): Created history item

`PUT /api/history/:id`
- Request: Partial update fields
- Response (200): Updated item

`DELETE /api/history/:id`
- Response (204): No content

**Watchlist**

`GET /api/watchlist`
`POST /api/watchlist`
`DELETE /api/watchlist/:id`

**AI Proxy**

`POST /api/ai/chat`
- Proxies to Python AI service
- Adds user context (history, preferences) before forwarding

#### ML Features Implementation

**Conversational Recommendations**:
- Input: User message + watch history + preferences
- Process: LLM with structured prompt containing user context
- Output: 3-5 recommendations with reasoning

**Episode Recaps**:
- Input: Show ID + user's current episode
- Process: Fetch episode data from TMDb → LLM summarization with spoiler awareness

### 5.5 Third-Party Integrations

- Purpose: Content metadata, images, episode data
- Rate Limits: 40 requests/10 seconds
- Fallback: Redis cache for popular content

**OpenAI API**
- Purpose: Chat responses, recap generation
- Rate Limits: Model-dependent
- Fallback: Degraded mode with pre-cached responses

**JustWatch API** (via unofficial)
- Purpose: Streaming availability
- Fallback: Manual lookup message
